The idea

The Gulf's most fashionable clients, and the millions of wealthy tourists who shop in Dubai every year, already own the big jewellery houses. What they can't easily find is the next name. AMANN is a small, very stylish multibrand house with a British soul, selling ultra-fashionable high-end jewellery and accessories by independent designers from around the world. Stephen Webster curates the selection and joins as an equity partner. ITALDIZAIN finds the sites and runs the stores. One flagship in Dubai first, then 18 stores across the GCC within ten years.

Our customers

Residents bring loyalty, tourists bring volume

I

The local collector

Young Gulf women buying for themselves, Gulf men (rings, bracelets, cufflinks) and affluent expats. They return for every new arrival and keep one consultant.

Wins with: new pieces, a client book, private previews

II

The gift and occasion buyer

Ramadan and Eid, weddings, Valentine's Day, anniversaries. Wants a clear price, beautiful packaging and a quick decision; often a man buying for a woman.

Wins with: seasonal gift capsules, strong entry pieces

III

The visiting luxury shopper

Tourists from Russia, Saudi Arabia and the GCC, China, India, Western Europe and the CIS: 16%, 11%, 10% and 8% of Dubai's tourist tax-free fashion spend come from Russia, Saudi Arabia, China and India.

Wins with: stock on hand, Russian, Chinese and Arabic-speaking staff, tax-free
